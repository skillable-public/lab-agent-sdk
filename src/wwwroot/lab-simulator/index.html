Lab Agent SDK - Lab simulator

This simulator embeds a Lab Agent iframe in a simulated Skillable lab and speaks protocol version 2 to it. Test your own agent, or open the sample agent that ships in this repository.

Test your Lab Agent

Embed your own agent in the simulated lab to drive it through the handshake and the postMessage contract.

Must be served over HTTPS in production. Localhost / dev URLs are fine while you're building.

Try the sample agent

Open the simulated lab with the sample agent from src/wwwroot/sample-agent/: a mentor in about 300 lines of plain JavaScript.

  • Does the handshake, declaring mentor mode.
  • Shows a chat, takes screenshots when the policy allows, and answers refusals.
  • Scripted replies, no model, no backend.
